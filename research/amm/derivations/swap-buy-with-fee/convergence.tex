\subsubsection{Equation~\ref{eqn:swap-buy-with-fee-convergence} derivation}%
\label{sssec:equation-eqn-swap-buy-with-fee-converge-derivation}

Note that the common term $Q$ from Equation~\ref{eqn:swap-buy-with-fee-slippage-input}
effectively denotes the amount of quote reserves corresponding to $b_i$ at $p_s$, less
the fee rate. Notably, this yields $C \geq 0$ when $p_s \geq p_m$.
