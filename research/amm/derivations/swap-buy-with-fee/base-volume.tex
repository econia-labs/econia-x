\subsubsection{Equation~\ref{eqn:swap-buy-with-fee-base-volume} derivation}%
\label{sssec:equation-eqn-swap-buy-with-fee-base-volume-derivation}

The base volume for a swap with a fee is the same as the output amount for a swap
without a fee, so simply substitute $b_v$ for $b_{out}$ in
Equation~\ref{eqn:swap-buy-base-out-solved}.

\begin{equation}
  b_v = \frac{b_i \cdot q_{in}}{q_i + q_{in}} \nonumber
\end{equation}
